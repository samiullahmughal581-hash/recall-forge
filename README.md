## 🌟 Welcome to MnemoForge

MnemoForge is a thoughtfully engineered memory cultivation studio — a place where the raw ore of facts is hammered, tempered, and refined into knowledge that stays with you for the long haul. Inspired by the timeless craft of memory training, this project reimagines what a learning companion can be in 2026: not a flashcard app, not a rote drill, but a living, breathing forge where every synapse you sharpen becomes part of a broader cognitive architecture.

If you have ever tried to memorize chemical definitions, foreign vocabulary, anatomical terms, programming syntax, or historical dates — and felt the frustration of forgetting them a week later — MnemoForge was built with you in mind. ## 🔥 Why MnemoForge Exists

Memory is not a warehouse. It is a garden — and like any garden, it demands cultivation, seasons of rest, and thoughtful pruning. Most tools treat memory as a bucket you fill and hope does not leak. MnemoForge treats it as an ecosystem.

Traditional memorization tools focus on quantity: how many cards can you review in a session, how many facts can you stuff into an hour. MnemoForge instead asks: how deeply can you encode this idea, and how gracefully will it return to you in three months when you actually need it? That simple reframing changes everything — from the pacing of reviews, to the way cards are authored, to how the system decides what you should see next.

The project began as a personal response to a very human problem: forgetting. It grew into a robust, cross-disciplinary toolkit used by language learners, chemistry students, medical trainees, and lifelong learners of all ages.

---

## 🧭 Core Philosophy

- **Retention over volume.** A single well-encoded idea beats ten shallow ones.
- **Context is king.** Facts learned in context survive far longer than isolated trivia.
- **Feedback is a mirror.** Progress should be visible, honest, and motivating.
- **The learner owns the data.** Your decks, your history, your insights — portable and yours.
- **Calm software.** No streak-shaming, no dark patterns, no guilt-driven engagement loops.

Every design decision in MnemoForge traces back to one of these five principles.

## 🎓 Learning Modes

MnemoForge ships with several distinct modes, each suited to a different cognitive task:

1. **Recall Mode** — classic prompt-and-response, ideal for definitions and vocabulary.
2. **Cloze Mode** — fill-in-the-blank sentences for grammar and contextual learning.
3. **Recognition Mode** — multiple-choice for early exposure to unfamiliar material.
4. **Interleaved Mode** — mixes related topics to strengthen discrimination between similar concepts.
5. **Exam Simulation** — timed, randomized sessions that mimic real test conditions.
6. **Free Review** — browse decks without pressure when you simply want exposure.

Switching between modes is effortless, and the engine remembers your preferences per deck.

## 🗂️ Deck Anatomy

Every deck is composed of cards, and every card is a small, structured artifact:

- **Front** — the prompt, question, or term.
- **Back** — the answer, definition, or translation.
- **Tags** — optional labels for filtering (e.g., chapter, difficulty, topic).
- **Notes** — personal mnemonics, imagery, or etymology.
- **Media** — optional images, audio clips, or formulas.
- **History** — every review is timestamped and scored, feeding the scheduler.

Decks can be imported, exported, merged, and shared as plain text for maximum portability.

## ⚙️ Adaptive Scheduling Engine

At the heart of MnemoForge is a scheduling engine inspired by decades of cognitive science research. Each card carries an internal model of how well you know it, how quickly you forget it, and how urgently it deserves your attention. Rather than a rigid calendar, the engine produces a dynamic queue that reshuffles itself daily.

The result is a rhythm that feels intuitive: difficult items appear more often, comfortable items drift politely into the background, and nothing slips through the cracks unnoticed.

## 📈 Progress Analytics

Numbers alone rarely motivate. MnemoForge’s analytics focus on stories — how your retention is trending, which decks are thriving, where your forgetting curve is steepest. Visual dashboards include:

The aim is not to overwhelm you with data, but to give you one honest, actionable insight per session.

## 🛠️ Under the Hood

MnemoForge is engineered as a modular system with a clear separation between the scheduler, storage layer, UI, and import/export pipelines. This makes it approachable for contributors and extensible for advanced users who want to plug in their own algorithms or data formats.

Key architectural themes:

- Deterministic scheduler with pluggable scoring functions.
- Local-first data model, synced opportunistically.
- Typed interfaces between subsystems.
- Comprehensive test coverage across scheduling and storage.
- Forward-compatible file formats.
